The Story

From refinery floors to enterprise platforms - the long way round.

My career began as a Mechanical Engineer at ExxonMobil in Kuala Lumpur - delivering engineering analysis and reliability work across more than 40 global refinery and chemical sites, contributing to a $3M refinery retrofit project, and presenting operational performance data to refining and chemical leadership. That foundation in code-compliant, high-stakes technical analysis shaped how I approach enterprise software today: rigorous, traceable, and outcome-focused.

I transitioned into IT business analysis and enterprise platform delivery, working across banking, fintech, healthcare, logistics, renewable energy, government systems, and digital platforms. Today I lead requirements engineering for a large-scale enterprise cloud transformation program at a major Canadian financial institution - supporting modernisation initiatives impacting approximately 30,000 users across Microsoft 365, Azure, IAM, WAF, and FinOps governance.

Earlier, I led requirements management for the Electronic Fiscal Device Management System (EFDMS), a national fiscal compliance platform serving 30,000+ retail outlets and architected to scale to 600,000+ devices. I've also led operations and platform development for a digital healthcare logistics startup, scaling it to 14,000+ orders and ~$150K cumulative revenue - and led a ~200-person engineering and field operations team deploying 18MW+ of solar infrastructure across 247 sites in Bangladesh.

I'm particularly interested in Senior Business Analyst, Operations Analyst, and consulting-style roles where complex systems, cross-functional teams, and data-driven decision making intersect. Currently advancing CCBA® certification (IIBA) and French proficiency for the Montreal market.
