MSOT Integrity Programme

A global engineering programme coordinated across three continents

The Minimum Safe Operating Temperature (MSOT) integrity programme was a global initiative evaluating over 100 refinery assets - heat exchangers, distillation towers, fractionation columns, and pressure equipment - across 40+ sites in the Americas.

The coordination challenge was significant: engineers distributed across Malaysia, Europe, and North America needed to function as a continuous delivery unit. A 24-hour cross-time-zone workflow model was established so that engineering analysis moved forward around the clock without gaps.

The programme was delivered $50K under a $300K SAP WBS budget - a result of tight scope management, workflow efficiency, and cross-regional coordination discipline.

Capital Projects & Engineering Specifications

Major refinery specifications authored from first principles

Primary Capital Project

Catalyst Bed Retrofit - Hydroprocessing Unit

Authored the full Mechanical Design Specification (M-Spec) for a catalyst bed retrofit on a kerosene hydroprocessing unit - defining mechanical design requirements, operating conditions, integrity considerations, and retrofit specifications.

This is primary engineering authorship on a capital project - not support work. Every parameter in the specification carried integrity and safety implications.

~$3M capital project value
Process Unit Specification

Catalyst Transfer System - FCC Unit

Contributed to M-Spec work on catalyst transfer piping systems for a Fluidised Catalytic Cracking (FCC) unit - high-temperature, high-pressure process equipment operating at the limits of materials performance.

FCC catalyst transfer lines carry eroded catalyst particles at extreme conditions - mechanical specification here requires deep understanding of erosion, fatigue, and dynamic loading.

FCC unit - major refinery process
Preliminary Design

Fluid Coking Gasifier - Flexicoking Unit

Supported preliminary engineering design and sizing work for a Flexicoking gasifier unit - a thermal cracking process that converts heavy residual oil fractions into lighter products and synthetic gas.

Preliminary design at this scale requires integrating process conditions, material constraints, and regulatory codes before detailed engineering begins.

Large-scale refinery process unit

Mechanical Integrity & Technical Tools

The technical foundation behind the analytical credibility

Fitness-for-Service Assessments

Evaluated the continued operational viability of refinery assets past their original design life - pressure vessels, distillation towers, venturi systems, heat exchangers, tanks, and piping under API and ASME codes.

FFS work requires integrating materials data, inspection findings, and operating history into a formal engineering judgment. It is inherently risk-based and requires structured analytical thinking under uncertainty.

PV Elite CAESAR II API 579 ASME B31.3

Piping Integrity & Process Analysis

Flow restriction, vibration, and fatigue analysis on naphtha hydrofining process unit piping using BreakX simulation software. Hot tap engineering analysis under high-stress, high-pressure conditions requiring rapid and accurate assessment.

Reactor nozzle stress evaluation, heat exchanger mechanical analysis, and FCC catalyst transfer line work - each requiring command of ASME B16.5 flange standards and API pressure codes.

BreakX (Simulation) ASME B16.5 API Codes PVElite

Engineering Standards Authorship

Contributed to global engineering documentation used across ExxonMobil operations - Tank Maintenance Guide major section rewrite, General Practice guideline updates, and Equipment Degradation Documentation.

Engineering guidance at this level has a global audience. The discipline of writing technical standards - precise, unambiguous, and defensible - directly transfers to BA artefact authorship.

Refinery Turnaround - Fawley, UK

Supported shutdown and turnaround engineering activities at Fawley Refinery, one of the UK's largest oil refineries. Turnaround environments are defined by extreme time pressure - every hour of downtime carries major financial impact.

Rapid calculation, clear communication, and the ability to make defensible engineering decisions under pressure in this environment are the same cognitive skills applied in high-stakes BA delivery today.
