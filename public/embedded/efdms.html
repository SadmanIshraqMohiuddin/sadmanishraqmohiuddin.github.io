The Problem

Bangladesh's VAT system ran entirely on manual bookkeeping

Every business with a trade licence - every Business Identification Number (BIN) holder - was legally required to collect VAT from customers and remit it to the government. But the entire process was manual: handwritten ledgers, paper receipts, and self-reported tax submissions that were impossible to verify at scale.

The National Board of Revenue needed a centralised, automated fiscal compliance system that could reach every retail outlet in the country - from corner shops to major superstore chains - and transmit every transaction to the government in real time.

📒

Fully Manual VAT Reporting

Business Identification Number (BIN) holders maintained handwritten transaction records. No standardisation, no auditability, and significant scope for under-reporting.

🔌

No Existing Digital Infrastructure

Small retailers had no POS systems. Large stores had existing POS but no government-compliant integration layer. Two entirely different solutions were required.

📡

Connectivity & Security Challenges

Devices needed to communicate securely with NBR's central backend across Bangladesh's cellular network - without exposing sensitive fiscal data to open networks.

⚡

Offline Resilience Required

Retailers couldn't be blocked from transacting during network outages. The system needed to work offline and sync reliably when connectivity returned.

EFD - Electronic Fiscal Device

For retailers without existing POS

Small shops, independent traders, and any Business Identification Number (BIN) holder without an existing point-of-sale system. The EFD provides a complete fiscal device - inventory management, sales processing, VAT calculation, and invoice generation - all in one government-mandated unit.

Full POS functionality built into the device
Connects directly to NBR backend via custom APN
Offline-first with automatic sync on reconnection
Covers the mass market - majority of Business Identification Number (BIN) holders
SDC - Sales Data Controller

For large stores with existing POS

Major superstores (Meena Bazar, Shwapno, UniMart) already had enterprise POS systems. Replacing these was impractical. The SDC sits between the existing POS and NBR - acting as a compliance aggregator that intercepts every transaction, validates it, and forwards it to the government backend.

Middleware integration - no POS replacement required
REST API server hosted on the SDC device for third-party POS integration
Third-party POS vendors integrate via government-compliant API specification
Handles bulk inventory - large product catalogues supported

Scope Management

Nine significant features delivered outside original scope

A formal scope expansion document was produced tracking every out-of-scope feature. Each was assessed, prioritised, and delivered without disrupting the core programme timeline.

OOS-01

Operator Selection

Implemented multi-Telco operator selection (Major National Telcos) across device setup, sign-in, and settings screens.

OOS-02

Database Migration

Built migration path from the existing legacy database - importing deviceInfo, shopInfo, invoices, and items into the new platform.

OOS-03

Invoice Reprint & Exchange Reprint

Full dashboard modules for reprinting invoices and exchange invoices - including preview, search, and print functionality.

OOS-04

Failed Invoice Management

Automated detection of invoices with 3+ failed sync attempts. Differentiated handling for new vs legacy platform invoices with intelligent retry logic.

OOS-05

Draft Sales Module

Full draft sale capability - insert, delete, list incomplete sales, and resume a draft sale from the cart screen.

OOS-06

Bulk Item Upload

CSV-based bulk item upload with downloadable template, validation, and batch item creation in the system.

OOS-07

Bluetooth & WiFi Printing

PDF printing via Bluetooth and WiFi across cart, invoice reprint, exchange, and X/Z report screens.

OOS-08

Sales Reporting Suite

SalesReport and SalesSummary modules with date/month/year/user/session filtering, PDF preview and printing.

OOS-09

App Localisation

Full English/Bangla language localisation via sidebar language selector - critical for field adoption across Bangladesh.
