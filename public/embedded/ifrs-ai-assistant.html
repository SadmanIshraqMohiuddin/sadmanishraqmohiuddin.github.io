The Problem

Compliance teams couldn't trust AI to interpret accounting law

International financial reporting standards span thousands of clauses across 40+ standards. Compliance teams were spending hours manually cross-referencing regulations — and when they turned to generic AI tools, results were dangerously unreliable.

The client needed a system that could answer IFRS questions with the same rigour as a qualified accountant, but at machine speed. A misapplied standard means regulatory exposure, audit failure, and financial restatements.

⏰

Manual Search Overhead

Analysts spending 3–6 hours per complex IFRS query manually cross-referencing clause hierarchies and linked standards.

⚠

Hallucination Risk

General-purpose LLMs cited non-existent IFRS clauses or conflated standards — unusable in a regulated environment without auditability.

🔗

No Cross-Standard Traceability

Standards like IFRS 16 reference IAS 36, IAS 37, IAS 16 — existing tools couldn't follow the full dependency chain.

📋

No Audit Trail

Compliance decisions needed traceability to exact IFRS wording — not paraphrases — for external auditor review.

My Role
